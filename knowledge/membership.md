# Tempo Fitness Membership

## Trial Class Pricing

Trial options:

- 1-for-1 Trial: SGD 35 - 50
- 3-Class Trial Pack: SGD 60 - 89

The 3-Class Trial Pack is usually valid for 2-3 weeks.

Users should contact staff to confirm the exact current trial price, eligibility, and validity.

## Drop-In Class

Single drop-in class:

- SGD 35 - 45 per class

Users should contact staff to confirm the exact current drop-in price.

## Class Packs

Typical class pack options:

- 10-class pack: SGD 320 - 380, valid for 3 months
- 20-class pack: SGD 600 - 700, valid for 6 months
- 50-class pack: SGD 1300 - 1500, valid for 12 months

Class packs generally support sharing, but users should confirm current sharing rules with staff.

## Monthly Passes

Typical monthly options:

- Unlimited Pass: SGD 280 - 380 per month
- Limited monthly pass, such as 8 classes per month: SGD 200 - 240 per month

Unlimited monthly passes usually require a 3, 6, or 12 month recurring billing contract.

Users should contact staff to confirm exact current pricing, contract length, billing date, and cancellation terms.

## Payment Methods

Payment methods have not been confirmed in the knowledge base.

Do not claim that Tempo Fitness accepts a specific payment method unless staff confirms it.

## Refund And Transfer Policy

All purchased trial classes, drop-in classes, class packs, and monthly passes are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable.

Special cases, such as serious injury or relocation out of Singapore, require valid official documentation, such as a medical certificate or company relocation proof.

Special cases are reviewed by management case by case and may involve an administrative fee.

Refunds, billing disputes, and policy exceptions require human handoff.
