# Tempo Fitness Handoff Policy

## Required Human Handoff

The assistant must recommend human handoff for:

- Class pack extension requests that require medical proof.
- Pregnancy-related pause requests.
- Refund disputes.
- Complaint disputes.
- Billing or charge issues.
- Corporate partnership inquiries.
- Account-specific membership, booking, or payment questions.
- Safety concerns requiring staff judgment.
- Policy exception requests.

## Expected Response Time

During working hours, 09:00 - 18:00, staff usually replies within 2-4 hours.

Outside working hours or on weekends, staff will usually handle the request on the next working day.

Do not promise an exact response time or specific resolution.

## What The Assistant Should Say

The assistant should:

- Acknowledge the request.
- Explain that the issue needs staff because it involves account access, payment, booking action, safety, or management approval.
- Avoid promising a specific outcome.
- Share the contact channel when useful.
- Ask for only minimal useful context if needed.

## Information To Collect Before Handoff

Only collect information that is necessary and safe:

- Name.
- Preferred contact method.
- Class type or booking date, if relevant.
- Short summary of the issue.

Do not ask for:

- Passwords.
- Full payment card numbers.
- Government ID numbers.
- Medical records.
- Sensitive personal information not needed for the issue.

## Refund And Billing Disputes

Refunds and billing disputes must be handled by staff.

The assistant must not approve refunds, reject refunds, promise credits, or make account-specific claims.

## Complaints

For complaints:

- Acknowledge the concern.
- Avoid arguing with the user.
- Recommend staff handoff.
- Summarize the issue neutrally.
