## What Classes Does Tempo Fitness Offer?

Tempo Fitness offers:

- Cycling class
- Yoga class
- HIIT class
- Night run

## How Long Is Each Class?

Each class is 45 to 60 minutes, including warm-up and stretching.

## What Is The Class Schedule?

Typical class windows:

- Weekday morning: 06:45 / 07:30
- Weekday lunch: 12:15 / 13:00
- Weekday evening: 18:15 / 19:15 / 20:15
- Weekend classes: between 08:30 and 16:00

Specific weekly schedules and available slots should be confirmed with staff.

## What Should I Bring For My First Class?

Bring comfortable workout clothes, appropriate training shoes, and water.

For cycling, users should confirm with staff whether special cycling shoes are required or provided.

## Are Classes Suitable For Beginners?

Yes, beginners can start with a manageable class.

Yoga or cycling is usually a better first choice than HIIT. HIIT is higher intensity and may be better after the user is comfortable with the studio format.

## Do I Need To Book In Advance?

Yes. Users should book in advance through the official app, official website, or partner platforms such as ClassPass.

Walk-ins are allowed only if there is an available slot and are not guaranteed.

## What Is The Cancellation Policy?

Tempo Fitness follows a 12-hour cancellation policy.

- More than 12 hours before class: free cancellation and class credit returned.
- Within 12 hours: late cancel and class credit deducted.
- No-show: class credit deducted.

Unlimited pass users may be charged an additional SGD 10 - 15 penalty for late cancel or no-show.

## Can I Join If I Am Late?

Late arrival is not allowed.

If the user arrives after class starts or more than 5 minutes late, the coach has the right to refuse entry. The class will be treated as a no-show and the class credit will be deducted.

## Are Purchases Refundable?

All purchased trial classes, single classes, class packs, and monthly passes are non-refundable and non-transferable.

Special cases, such as serious injury or relocation out of Singapore, require valid official proof and management approval case by case. An administrative fee may apply.
