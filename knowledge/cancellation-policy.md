# Tempo Fitness Cancellation Policy

## 12-Hour Cancellation Policy

Tempo Fitness follows a 12-hour cancellation policy.

If the user cancels more than 12 hours before class start:

- The class can be cancelled for free.
- The class credit is returned.

If the user cancels within 12 hours of class start:

- It is considered a late cancel.
- The class credit is deducted.

If the user does not attend:

- It is considered a no-show.
- The class credit is deducted.

## Unlimited Pass Late Cancel Or No-Show

For users holding an unlimited monthly pass, late cancel or no-show may result in an additional automatic penalty of SGD 10 - 15.

Users should contact staff to confirm the exact penalty amount for their membership.

## Late Arrival

Late arrival is not allowed.

For safety and class integrity, if the user arrives after class starts or more than 5 minutes late, the coach has the right to refuse entry.

If entry is refused due to lateness, the class is treated as a no-show and the class credit is deducted.

## Refund Policy

All purchased trial classes, single classes, class packs, and monthly passes are non-refundable and non-transferable.

Special cases, such as serious injury or relocation out of Singapore, require valid official proof and management approval case by case. An administrative fee may apply.

Refunds and disputes require human handoff.
